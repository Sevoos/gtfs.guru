Diagnosis

What this notice means

First and last stop of a trip must define both arrival_time and departure_time fields. Per [stop_times.txt](http://gtfs.org/reference/static/#stop_timestxt), "If there are not separate times for arrival and departure at a stop, enter the same value for arrival_time and departure_time."

Operational effect

Why it matters

The first and last stop time of every trip must carry both an arrival and a departure time, because they anchor the trip in the timetable. Without them, consumers cannot tell when the trip starts or ends and may drop it.

Diagnosis

What usually causes it

  • A timetable that stores only timing points, with the terminus not marked as one.
  • An export that blanks arrival_time at the first stop and departure_time at the last stop as a matter of principle — GTFS requires both at both ends.
  • Stop times reordered so that a row without times became the trip's first or last.
  • A trip extended with a new terminus whose times were not entered.

Repair

How to fix it

Fill in `arrival_time` and `departure_time` on the first and last rows of the trip, in `stop_sequence` order. Intermediate stops may leave both blank so that consumers interpolate them, but the two edges may not.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stop_times.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Apply the repair described above to that record, and leave unrelated rows alone.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that missing_trip_edge no longer appears for the record.

Do not do this: Do not copy the neighbouring stop's time into the edge. That publishes a departure the vehicle does not make; if the real time is unknown, find it rather than inventing one.
