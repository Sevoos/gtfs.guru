Diagnosis

What this notice means

The values in the given column of the input rows are out of range.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The value is numeric but outside the range the specification allows for the field, so the record cannot be interpreted. Consumers drop the row, and a dropped stop or stop time removes service.

Scope

Affected files and report fields

The affected file is determined at validation time. Use the filename or related context field in your report to locate it.

Diagnosis

What usually causes it

  • Latitude and longitude swapped, which pushes the latitude past 90 anywhere outside the tropics.
  • Coordinates exported in a projected system (metres) instead of WGS 84 degrees.
  • A decimal separator lost, turning 35.17 into 3517.
  • A negative or zero value where the field requires a positive one, such as a headway of 0.

Repair

How to fix it

Check the field's permitted range in the GTFS reference and correct the value at source. Latitudes run from -90 to 90 and longitudes from -180 to 180; `stop_sequence`, headways and counts must be non-negative.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stops.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Apply the repair described above to that record, and leave unrelated rows alone.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that number_out_of_range no longer appears for the record.

Do not do this: Do not clamp an out-of-range coordinate to the nearest legal value. A latitude forced from 3517 down to 90 is a valid coordinate at the North Pole, and the feed will pass this check while pointing riders at the Arctic.
