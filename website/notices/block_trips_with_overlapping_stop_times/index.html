Diagnosis

What this notice means

Trips with the same block id have overlapping stop times.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A block tells riders they can stay on the vehicle between trips. Two trips in one block that run at the same time on the same service day cannot both be served by that vehicle, so in-seat transfers and vehicle assignment break. The report's `intersection` is the first service date (YYYYMMDD) on which both trips are active. A trip whose last stop repeats the exact arrival/departure pair of the next trip's first stop is a block transfer, not an overlap; trips whose services share no active date never overlap, even under the same service_id.

Repair

How to fix it

Fix the stop times of one of the trips, move it to another block, or give the two trips services that do not run on the same dates.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stop_times.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Apply the repair described above to that record, and leave unrelated rows alone.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that block_trips_with_overlapping_stop_times no longer appears for the record.
