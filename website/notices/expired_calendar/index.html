Diagnosis

What this notice means

This warning takes into account the calendar_dates.txt file as well as the calendar.txt file.

Operational effect

Why it matters

Every service this `service_id` defines has already ended, counting `calendar.txt` and `calendar_dates.txt` together. Trips on it run on no future date, so as far as any rider is concerned that service no longer exists.

Scope

Affected files and report fields

Diagnosis

What usually causes it

  • A feed published on schedule but built from a timetable extract that was not rolled forward.
  • A seasonal or school service left in the feed after its last day.
  • calendar.txt updated for the new period while calendar_dates.txt exceptions were left on the old one, or the reverse.
  • An automated export pinned to a fixed date range rather than to the current one.

Check both sides. Look at calendar_dates.txt as well — an added date there can keep a service alive past its calendar.txt end_date, and this check already accounts for that.

Repair

How to fix it

Extend the service into the period the feed is meant to cover, or remove the service and the trips that depend on it if it really has been withdrawn. A feed should normally be republished before its service periods lapse.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open calendar.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Apply the repair described above to that record, and leave unrelated rows alone.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that expired_calendar no longer appears for the record.

Do not do this: Do not push end_date years into the future to stop the warning recurring. A service period that outlives the timetable it was built from publishes schedules you have not actually planned.
