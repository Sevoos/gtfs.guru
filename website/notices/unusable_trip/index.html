Diagnosis

What this notice means

A trip must visit more than one stop in stop_times.txt to be usable by passengers for boarding and alighting.

Operational effect

Why it matters

A trip with fewer than two stop times cannot be boarded and alighted, so no rider can ever use it. Journey planners ignore it, and it inflates the apparent size of the feed without adding service.

Scope

Affected files and report fields

  • stop_times.txtOpen GTFS specification
  • trips.txtOpen GTFS specification
Report fieldTypeMeaning
csvRowNumberintegerThe row number of the faulty record.
tripIdstringThe faulty record's id.

Diagnosis

What usually causes it

  • A truncated stop_times.txt export — a row limit, a failed write, or a filter that dropped part of the file.
  • Stop times filtered out by a foreign_key_violation on stop_id earlier in the pipeline, leaving the trip with one stop or none.
  • Deadhead or garage movements exported as passenger trips.
  • A trip duplicated from another one and saved before its stop times were entered.

Check both sides. Count the stop_times.txt rows for this trip_id. Zero rows usually means an export problem; exactly one usually means the rest of the trip was filtered out somewhere.

Repair

How to fix it

Add the missing `stop_times.txt` rows so the trip visits at least two stops, or remove the trip if it is not real service.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stop_times.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Apply the repair described above to that record, and leave unrelated rows alone.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that unusable_trip no longer appears for the record.

Do not do this: Do not add a second stop time at the same stop to reach the count of two. That produces a trip that goes nowhere and will be reported again for zero travel between consecutive stops.
