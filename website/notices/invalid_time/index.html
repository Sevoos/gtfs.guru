Diagnosis

What this notice means

Time must be in the H:MM:SS, HH:MM:SS or HHH:MM:SS format.

Example: 14:30:00 for 2:30PM or 25:35:00 for 1:35AM on the next day.

Operational effect

Why it matters

The value is not a GTFS time, so the arrival or departure it represents cannot be read. Consumers drop the stop time or the whole trip, which removes service from the feed.

Scope

Affected files and report fields

The affected file is determined at validation time. Use the filename or related context field in your report to locate it.

Report fieldTypeMeaning
This notice has no structured context fields.

Diagnosis

What usually causes it

  • A spreadsheet reformatted the column as a time or a duration and wrote 8:00 AM, 08:00 or a decimal fraction of a day.
  • Seconds omitted: 14:30 instead of 14:30:00.
  • Trips running past midnight written as 00:20:00, which places them at the start of the same service day instead of after it.
  • A locale that uses a comma or a dot as the separator instead of a colon.

Repair

How to fix it

Write times as `HH:MM:SS` on a 24-hour clock, counted from noon minus twelve hours of the service day. Times after midnight on a service that started the previous day continue past 24 — a trip leaving at 1:35 the next morning is `25:35:00`, not `01:35:00`.

  1. 1
    Locate the record

    Open stop_times.txt and use the report fields below to find the exact row.

  2. 2
    Change the source data

    Apply the repair described above to that record, and leave unrelated rows alone.

  3. 3
    Validate again

    Rebuild the GTFS archive and confirm that invalid_time no longer appears for the record.

Do not do this: Do not convert a past-midnight time to 00:20:00 to make it look valid. That is a syntactically correct time twenty-four hours away from the trip it belongs to, and it will reorder the trip's stops.
